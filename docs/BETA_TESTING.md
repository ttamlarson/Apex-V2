# OVERLOAD Beta Testing Programme

Welcome to the OVERLOAD beta. The goal is to make the app clear, reliable and useful on real devices before a wider release.

## How to take part

1. Open [OVERLOAD](https://ttamlarson.github.io/OVERLOAD/).
2. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome → menu → Install app.
3. Use your own test data. Do not enter passwords, private messages, or sensitive personal or health information into feedback.
4. Try the tasks below without someone coaching you through the interface.
5. Use the **Beta feedback** shortcut (or Settings → Beta testing programme) to submit what happened.

## Test missions

- [ ] First launch: can you understand what OVERLOAD does and where to begin?
- [ ] Install it to your home screen, close it, and reopen it.
- [ ] Create a programme, add a day and exercises, save it, then reopen and edit it.
- [ ] Start a workout, record sets/reps/weights, save it, and find it in history.
- [ ] Log a meal and find your daily calorie/protein totals.
- [ ] Find your progress, strength trends and personal records.
- [ ] Complete a recovery check-in and find it in history.
- [ ] Switch themes and navigate between sections.
- [ ] With the app loaded, briefly test airplane mode/offline use; reconnect afterwards.
- [ ] Export a backup. Do not test restore over important personal data unless you have a separate test profile and backup.

## What makes feedback useful?

For a bug, include the device/browser, what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, and whether you can reproduce it. For usability feedback, tell us what you were trying to do, where you hesitated, and what wording or layout would have helped. A screenshot is useful only if it contains no personal information.

## Privacy

OVERLOAD's beta form does not read your workout, nutrition, recovery, measurement, or other app records. It does not run analytics or silently upload reports. When you choose **Continue to GitHub issue**, the text you reviewed is sent to GitHub, where it becomes a repository issue visible to people with access to the public repository. Do not include sensitive or identifying information. You may instead copy the report and share it privately with the app owner.

## Testing principles

- Test on your normal phone and browser, not only a desktop.
- Try tasks without help; difficulty finding a feature is valuable feedback.
- Report one issue per submission where possible.
- Mention app version shown in the report.
- Keep a backup of important records before testing backup/restore or destructive actions.

Thanks for helping improve OVERLOAD.
